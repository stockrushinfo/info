Delete your account

How to delete your StockRush account and understand what happens to your data.

This action is irreversible through the app. Successful deletion removes your account and synced portfolio from the active database. Technical and provider-held records are subject to the retention conditions below.

Steps to delete your account

1
Open the menu
Tap the menu icon (☰) in the top-left corner of the app to open the drawer.
2
Go to Settings
In the drawer, tap Settings to open the settings screen.
3
Tap "Delete account"
Scroll to the bottom of the Settings screen and tap Delete account. A confirmation dialog will appear.
4
Confirm deletion
Tap Delete account to confirm. Successful deletion removes your account and synced portfolio from the active database and revokes your sessions. Technical records expire separately.

What gets deleted

The table describes successful account deletion. Device cleanup applies to the requesting device only; it does not remotely erase other devices or their backups.

Data Location Action
User account (Google ID / Apple ID, email, display name) Server Deleted immediately
Portfolio & benchmark synced for cross-device restore and notifications (tickers, quantities, invested amounts, purchase dates, dividends) Server Deleted immediately
Notification preferences & push notification tokens (FCM) Server Deleted immediately
Biometric login public key Server Deleted immediately
Biometric login private key Device (operating-system secure storage) Deleted immediately
Local portfolio copy & settings (currency, benchmark, country, preferences) Device Deleted immediately
Authentication tokens Device Deleted immediately
Widget cached data Device Deleted immediately
Uploaded broker files Server Processed for import; see Section 1.4

Data retention

Successful deletion removes the account and synced portfolio from the active database. App and widget sessions are revoked. Session records and token hashes remain until expiry and automatic cleanup. Deletion does not itself erase provider-held telemetry, infrastructure logs or backups. See Section 8 for retention.

Alternative: request deletion via email

If you can no longer access the app, you can request account deletion by sending an email to stockrushinfo@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account (Google or Apple). We will process your request within 30 days.

Privacy Policy

Last updated:

StockRush ("we", "us", or "our") operates the StockRush mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, storage, and disclosure of personal data when you use the App, and of your rights under applicable data protection law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

In short: we collect the minimum needed to run your account, and we store your portfolio on our servers to sync it, calculate performance and deliver optional notifications. We never sell your data or show ads. Account deletion and its retention limits are explained below.

1. Data we collect

1.1 Authentication data

We use Google Sign-In (Android only) and Apple Sign-In (iOS only) to authenticate users. When you sign in, we receive your email address and a unique identifier from the chosen provider. This data is used solely to create and manage your account within StockRush.

1.2 Data stored on our servers

We store the following data, associated with your account, on our servers:

  • Authentication provider identifier (Google ID or Apple ID)
  • Email address (to identify your account for support and deletion requests)
  • Display name (from your Google or Apple profile)
  • App and widget session records: refresh-token hashes, account and session identifiers, installation identifier, creation and expiry dates, and revocation status. These support authentication, token renewal and protection against token reuse.
  • Your portfolio and benchmark — see Section 1.3
  • Notification preference and push notification tokens — see Section 1.5
  • Biometric login public key, if you enable biometric login — see Section 1.6
  • A monthly counter of AI-parsing uses (to enforce usage limits — no file content)

1.3 Portfolio data & cross-device sync

Your portfolio is the core of the app, so we sync it to enable a seamless experience. When the app refreshes your live data, your holdings — ticker symbols, quantities, invested amounts, purchase dates, and accumulated dividends — together with your benchmark composition and your selected currency and country, are stored on our servers and linked to your account.

This enables portfolio performance calculations, charts and benchmark comparisons for the app and widget, as well as the following features:

  • Cross-device sync — when you sign in on a new device, your portfolio is automatically restored, so you don't have to re-enter it.
  • Daily performance notifications — if enabled, our server recalculates your portfolio's daily performance to send you the notification (see Section 1.5).

We keep an up-to-date portfolio snapshot per account. Read-only requests, including widget refreshes, do not overwrite it. We do not use your portfolio for advertising. Some interactions with positions and brokers are counted as analytics events — without ticker symbols or portfolio values — as described in Section 4. You can erase the active server portfolio copy by deleting your account (see Section 9).

1.4 Broker file upload & AI parsing

You may import your portfolio by uploading a broker statement file (Excel/CSV/PDF). The file is sent to our server solely to enable the import functionality of the app. We extract portfolio-related information such as ticker symbols, quantities, and transaction amounts, and return the structured data to your device.

For directly supported brokers (e.g., eToro, Trading 212), parsing is performed on our servers using predefined templates.

For unsupported brokers, the text extracted from your file may be sent to a third-party AI service (Anthropic Claude) to interpret the file structure and extract transaction data. This text may include personal information contained in your broker statement (e.g., name, account number). We do not control what data your broker includes in exported files. Anthropic processes the text under the terms and retention conditions applicable to its API service. We do not promise immediate deletion by Anthropic. For general information, see their Privacy Policy.

Uploaded files are used for processing, not kept as a broker-document archive. Processing may involve temporary upload storage. The application does not intentionally log file contents. For unsupported brokers, extracted text is securely sent to Anthropic for parsing, subject to the applicable API retention conditions. Avoid uploading unnecessary personal information. The resulting portfolio is handled as described in Section 1.3.

1.5 Push notifications

Daily performance notifications are optional and turned off by default. You can enable or disable them at any time in Settings → Notifications.

If you enable them, we store a push notification token (provided by Firebase Cloud Messaging) and your notification preference on our servers. Once a day, after market close, our server uses the portfolio stored under Section 1.3 to compute your portfolio's daily percentage change and how it compares to your benchmark, and sends you a notification. The notification contains only percentage values — it does not include your holdings, amounts, or other identifying details.

When you disable notifications, sign out, or delete your account, the associated token and preference are removed from our servers.

1.6 Biometric login

You can optionally enable biometric login (fingerprint or Face ID). When you do, a cryptographic key pair is generated on your device. The private key never leaves your device and is kept in the device's secure storage provided by the operating system (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). Hardware-backed protection depends on the device and implementation. Only the corresponding public key is sent to our servers, where it is used to verify a challenge-response at sign-in. Biometric data itself (your fingerprint or face) is never accessed by, or transmitted to, StockRush — it stays within your device's operating system.

1.7 Data stored on your device

A copy of your portfolio (tickers, quantities, amounts, dates, dividends), your settings, benchmarks, authentication tokens, and the biometric private key are stored locally on your device. An installation identifier may remain locally after account deletion. Data is transmitted for the purposes described throughout this policy, including authentication, analytics and widget refreshes.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not track your location
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or ad tracking
  • We do not sell personal data
  • We do not share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes
  • We do not use your portfolio data for profiling or automated decision-making

3. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to provide the App's core functionality: account management, authentication, and syncing/restoring your portfolio.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for optional features you explicitly enable, such as push notifications and biometric login, and for analytics where consent is required. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — to keep the App secure, stable, and free of bugs (e.g., crash reporting), balanced against your rights.

4. Third-party services

The App communicates with our backend to:

  • Authenticate users
  • Fetch market data (e.g. stock prices, exchange rates)
  • Sync and restore your portfolio
  • Process broker file uploads
  • Send daily performance notifications (if enabled)

We rely on third-party services including:

  • Google (authentication, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Apple (authentication via Sign in with Apple, and Apple Push Notification service on iOS)
  • Market data providers (for financial data)
  • Anthropic (AI-based parsing of unsupported broker files, as described in Section 1.4)

These services may receive limited technical data necessary to operate, such as device information, request metadata, push tokens, or ticker symbols.

Google Sign-In

Authentication is handled by Google Sign-In. We receive a user identifier and email address to enable account creation and authentication within the App. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

Apple Sign-In (iOS)

On iOS devices, authentication can also be handled by Sign in with Apple. We receive a user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your email address (which may be an Apple private relay address). Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Firebase Cloud Messaging

The App includes Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (and, on iOS, the Apple Push Notification service) to deliver notifications. When the App starts, the Firebase SDK can generate a Firebase installation identifier and a device push token and exchange them with Firebase, together with app and device configuration data, even if you have not enabled notifications. Only if you enable notifications is the push token sent to and stored on our servers, so we can send you the daily performance notification. See Firebase Privacy.

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports in order to improve app stability and fix bugs. This data may include device model, operating system version, app state, and stack traces. Crash reports are not designed to include directly identifying personal data, portfolio information, or financial data. For more information, see Firebase Privacy.

Analytics

We use analytics services such as Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App and to improve it. This may include app usage events such as app opens and sessions, session duration and device information, which the Firebase SDK collects automatically. Screen changes inside the App are not detected automatically by the SDK: the App sends its own navigation events, which say which tab is opened. The parameters that the App itself attaches to its events come from a closed list: the asset type (stock, ETF or crypto), the broker code selected in the App (eToro, Trading 212, Fineco, DEGIRO, other, none, manual entry, or all brokers when filtering), the login method (Google, Apple or biometric), the selected currency (US dollar, euro or pound sterling) and time period (day, week, month, year, year to date or all time), the screen or tab (portfolio, analytics or markets), on/off switches (notifications, biometric login), whether a position was entered by units or by amount, and counts (whole numbers, for example how many positions an import contains or how many remain after a removal). They never include ticker symbols, benchmark names, country, search text, amounts or other portfolio values. Analytics collection is enabled automatically in the current app configuration, with no separate in-app analytics consent switch. Aggregated reporting does not make the underlying event collection anonymous.

5. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (and in particular under the GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten") — available directly in-app via account deletion
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time for features based on consent (e.g. notifications)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at stockrushinfo@gmail.com. We respond to requests within 30 days.

6. Widget

The StockRush widget displays your portfolio performance on your home screen. The widget reads data stored locally on your device and periodically fetches updated prices and performance calculations from our server. Requests include local portfolio records and benchmark holdings, country, currency and chart-period parameters, and authentication credentials for a separate widget session. They are not limited to ticker symbols. Widget refreshes do not overwrite the saved server portfolio snapshot.

7. Data security

All communication between the App and our server is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Authentication tokens and your biometric private key are stored securely on your device. Access to data on our servers is restricted and authenticated. While no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

8. Data retention

Successful deletion removes the account record and synced portfolio from the active database and revokes app and widget sessions. Session records, including token hashes, account and installation identifiers, timestamps and revocation status, remain until their recorded expiry dates and automatic database cleanup. The repository configuration uses a seven-day refresh-token lifetime; later-issued tokens have their own expiry dates. Cleanup is asynchronous. Short-lived biometric challenges also expire automatically. Upload and AI processing are described in Section 1.4.

Account deletion does not automatically erase previously collected Firebase telemetry or other provider-held data. Applicable provider retention conditions also apply. We do not promise immediate erasure of infrastructure logs or backup copies. Contact us for retention information or to exercise deletion rights.

9. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the App (Settings → Delete account) or by contacting us at stockrushinfo@gmail.com.

Successful deletion removes the active account record, including provider identifiers, email, display name, notification preferences, push tokens and biometric public key, and the synced portfolio and benchmark. Sessions are revoked. Technical records and provider-held data are subject to the retention conditions in Section 8.

The deletion flow clears account-related local data on the requesting device, not other devices, exported files or backups. An installation identifier may remain locally. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your server account.

10. Children's privacy

StockRush is not intended for use by children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children.
